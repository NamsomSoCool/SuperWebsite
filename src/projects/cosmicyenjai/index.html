Overview

Cosmic YenJai is a bullet-hell prototype focused on readable chaos. The goal is to create a rhythm where players dance through patterns, use bursts of power, and keep momentum while the screen fills with high-contrast energy.

Key Features

Pattern-dense waves that reward micro-dodges and confident movement.
Risk-reward pickups that push aggressive play in tight moments.
A cosmic clown aesthetic with bold color passes and punchy impact FX.
