Game Project

Project Liminal

A two-player co-op puzzle game set inside a liminal art gallery where each player sees a different reality. Only communication, observation, and teamwork reveal what has shifted before time runs out.

FutureGames showcase 4 weeks production 6 people team
View on Itch

Overview

Project Liminal explores the tension of shared spaces with mismatched perception. Players are split into parallel versions of the gallery, hunting for subtle changes that only their partner can see. The experience is built to reward calm communication and fast pattern recognition.

My Role

Level design focused on landmark silhouettes and readable player paths.
Environment art pass for lighting contrast, mood, and focal points.
Prop dressing to support the “what changed?” gameplay hook.
