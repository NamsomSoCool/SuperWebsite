Gnome Splash

A kinetic co-op prototype where water is both your weapon and your movement tech. Chain boosts, launch friends, and keep the chaos flowing in tight, replayable arenas.

Arcade loop 3-week sprint Co-op chaos
View on Itch

Overview

Gnome Splash is a momentum-heavy prototype focused on short, repeatable runs. Players stitch together splash gadgets to keep speed, control space, and improvise their way through fast encounter loops. The tone is playful, but the mechanics reward sharp timing and clean routes.

Key Features

Splash tools that double as movement tech and crowd control.
Short arenas tuned for fast retries and score chasing.
Co-op friendly design with big readable effects and clear callouts.
