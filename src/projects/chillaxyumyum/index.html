Overview

Chillax Yum-Yum is a playful food game prototype built around timing, layering, and satisfying feedback. It aims for that "friend kitchen" vibe — a little mess, shared reactions, and big, goofy wins when the dish actually lands.

Core Loop

Prep ingredients by chopping, flipping, and seasoning.
Assemble orders under time pressure with clean visual cues.
Serve combos quickly to keep the kitchen streak alive.
